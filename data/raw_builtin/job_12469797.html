Circle (NYSE: CRCL) is one of the world's leading internet financial platform companies, building the foundation of a more open, global economy through digital assets, payment applications, and programmable blockchain infrastructure. Circle's platform includes the world's largest regulated stablecoin network anchored by USDC, Circle Payments Network for global money movement, and Arc, an enterprise-grade blockchain designed to become the Economic OS for the internet. Enterprises, financial institutions, and developers use Circle to power trusted, internet-scale financial innovation. Learn more at circle.com .
What you'll be part of:
Circle is committed to visibility and stability in everything we do. As we grow as an organization, we're expanding into some of the world's strongest jurisdictions. Speed and efficiency are motivators for our success and our employees live by our company values : High Integrity, Future Forward, Multistakeholder, Mindful, and Driven by Excellence. We have built a flexible work environment where new ideas are encouraged and everyone is a stakeholder.
What you'll be responsible for
As a Principal Product Manager on Circle's Platform team, you will shape the shared services that every Circle product is built on. The Platform team builds the reusable foundation behind Circle's stablecoin, payments, custody, and developer products - the capabilities that let product teams ship faster, give customers a consistent enterprise-grade experience, and let Circle scale without rebuilding the same infrastructure in every product.
You will identify where shared capabilities - identity & access management, enterprise controls and policy, wallets infrastructure, developer tooling, and the foundations for AI agents - create the most leverage, define strategy and roadmaps that balance consistency with flexibility, and drive adoption across product teams. You will also help define how Circle's platform serves a world where AI agents are first-class users - with the identity, scoped permissions, controls, and auditability that make autonomous activity safe.
What you'll work on
  • Define and own product strategy and multi-year roadmaps for core Platform services, connecting company priorities, product team needs, and customer outcomes into a clear point of view on what to build, what to standardize, and what to leave to product teams.
  • Define how the platform supports AI agents and agentic workflows, including agent identity, scoped and delegated permissions, spend and policy controls, human-in-the-loop approvals, and auditable automated actions.
  • Make Circle's platform agent-ready by design, so agents can discover, understand, and reliably use Circle products through well-structured APIs, MCP servers, tool definitions, and machine-readable documentation.
  • Build shared, self-serve platform capabilities that product teams can adopt with minimal custom engineering - with clear APIs, configuration, documentation, and service boundaries.
  • Lead customer and internal discovery with enterprises, developers, and Circle product teams to understand needs, validate solutions, and turn feedback into roadmap priorities.
  • Drive cross-product adoption and migration onto shared services, sequencing work so existing customers and products move with minimal disruption.
  • Partner deeply with engineering, design, security, compliance, legal, risk, finance, operations, and commercial teams to translate technical, regulatory, and business requirements into clear product requirements and executable plans.
  • Represent Platform priorities in senior product, engineering, and business forums, influencing tradeoffs across speed, security, reliability, customer experience, cost, and long-term leverage.
  • Contribute to platform domains such as wallets and key management, billing and monetization, reporting and data access, and developer settings, bringing a consistent model across products while supporting product-specific needs.
  • Define and report on platform success metrics, such as adoption by product teams, time to launch new products, reliability, operational efficiency, developer experience, and customer satisfaction.
  • Shape the enterprise foundation of Circle's products, including organizations and workspaces, identity and authentication, roles and permissions, approval policies, audit logging, and the controls enterprise and institutional customers expect.

What you'll bring to Circle
  • 10+ years of product management experience, including substantial experience building platform, infrastructure, developer, or enterprise products at a cloud, payments, fintech, or B2B SaaS company.
  • Breadth and domain expertise across core platform areas: such as identity and access management, multi-tenant account and organization models, authorization and policy, audit, data and reporting, and developer APIs.
  • Fluency in AI and agentic systems, and hands-on experience using AI tools for product discovery, prototyping, technical research, and workflow automation.
  • Strong technical fluency with distributed systems and platform architecture, including APIs and SDKs, event-driven systems, data models, service boundaries, reliability, and security, with the ability to reason through tradeoffs alongside engineers and architects.
  • Experience building enterprise administration and controls, such as SSO, RBAC, approval workflows, audit logs, or customer-facing reporting.
  • Proven ability to define strategy for horizontal platforms that serve many internal and external customers, and to turn ambiguous, cross-team needs into clear priorities, roadmaps, and measurable outcomes.
  • A track record of driving adoption of shared services across multiple product teams, including migrating existing customers and products onto new platforms.
  • Ability to operate across long-term platform strategy and near-term execution, especially in environments with complex dependencies, many internal customers, and competing priorities.
  • Strong business judgment, with the ability to connect platform investments to product velocity, customer adoption, revenue, operational cost, risk posture, and competitive position.
  • Experience partnering with engineering, security, compliance, legal, risk, finance, and commercial teams on products where security, reliability, regulatory readiness, and customer trust are non-negotiable.
  • Demonstrated leadership influencing executives and senior cross-functional stakeholders through crisp written narratives, strategic tradeoff analysis, and clear decision framing.
  • Highly analytical and metrics-driven, with experience defining platform health, adoption, and operational KPIs.
